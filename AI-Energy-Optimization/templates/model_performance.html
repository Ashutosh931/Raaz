Viva Cheat Sheet: Key ML Concepts in this Project
Why Random Forest? Random Forest is an ensemble learning method that builds 100 decision trees and averages their predictions. It easily models non-linear relationships (e.g. exponential AC cooling demand above 28°C) and prevents overfitting through bootstrap aggregation (bagging).
What does R² Score mean? The Coefficient of Determination (\(R^2\)) measures the proportion of variance in energy consumption explained by the features. Our \(R^2 = {{ (rf.r2 * 100) | round(1) }}\%\) indicates superior predictive precision.
What is MAE vs RMSE? MAE (Mean Absolute Error) is the average absolute difference between predicted and actual values. RMSE penalizes larger prediction errors more heavily by squaring deviations before taking the root.
