# Problem Statement

## 1. Background
In contemporary urban households, electricity is one of the highest recurring monthly utility expenses. However, electricity consumption remains largely invisible to consumers until they receive their monthly utility bill. By then, it is impossible to undo the excess power consumed or understand which specific appliances, hours of the day, or behaviors caused the bill to spike.

## 2. Key Challenges Identified
1. **Lack of Predictive Foresight**: Consumers cannot anticipate the electrical load of running high-draw appliances (such as air conditioners, water heaters, and washing machines) under fluctuating ambient temperatures and occupancy levels.
2. **Peak Grid Strain & Surges**: Electric utilities experience extreme demand spikes during evening peak windows (18:00 to 22:00). High coincident usage during these windows overburdens the grid, increases generation costs, and triggers higher tariff brackets.
3. **Vampire / Standby Power Losses**: Unmonitored consumer electronics continuously consume standby power (accounting for 8% to 10% of residential bills), which goes completely unnoticed by average consumers.
4. **Generic, Non-Actionable Advice**: Standard utility tips (e.g., "turn off lights") lack mathematical justification, context-awareness, and financial projection.
5. **Data Inaccessibility**: While digital meters collect interval data, consumers lack intuitive tools to upload, clean, visualize, and extract predictive intelligence from their own consumption records.

## 3. Solution Overview
The **AI-Based Smart Energy Consumption Prediction and Optimization System** resolves these challenges by bridging Machine Learning prediction models with intuitive web interfaces, transforming raw meter data into predictive foresight, live cost calculations, and proactive behavioral guidance.
